# Plan: every LN Guide page in plain voice

## Goal

Every page LN Guide publishes reads like someone sitting beside the reader,
showing them what to do: calm, clear and specific. The voice is atlas's
`standards/prose.md`, section 3, and Ship from stock is the finished example.

## Decisions

- **Guide by guide, each rewritten then walked in LN before the next.** A
  reworded task loses its walked mark, so walking straight after the rewrite
  keeps at most one guide unwalked at a time.
- **Every guide is walked in full** by an agent in rux's Chrome, in the
  training company, even where it creates master data there.
- **rux approves each guide after its walk**, and the next guide starts only
  then.
- **What no walk covers is rewritten freely**: the ideas pages, each screen's
  purpose line, and side tasks with no steps.
- **Exact LN labels stay exact**, and a sentence that is unsure says so.
