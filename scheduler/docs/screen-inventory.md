# Screen inventory

The surfaces of the retired trips app, `rux-ui`, each with a verdict and,
for a keep, what it is in this app. The old app is a
floating-window desktop; this one is a Carbon page, so every movable window
becomes a panel, a modal or a page.

Verdicts: **keep** builds next, **later** builds after the record tables work,
**decide** is open, **drop** is not rebuilt. A drop can be reopened; the commit
that reopens it says why.

A *template* is one of the twelve in `design/templates/`; a *module* is one
of Design's `js/*.js` behaviours; an *app component* is this app's own.

## 1. Views

| Old view | Verdict | Becomes |
|---|---|---|
| Customers | keep | `contacts.html`, the Contacts list, and one contact at `contacts.html?id=` (§7). |
| Documents | keep | `documents.html`, the office's own paperwork, such as insurance certificates, the W-9 and a driver's background check form, in a data table with search, where every word typed has to be in the row's kind, holder, customer, driver, file name or note, or in the name of a customer that is part of the row's customer, so a school's name finds its district's documents. A row shows its kind over its status and note, who it is for, the holder named on it over its customer, and the day it ends, the earliest of its parts'. The status, worked out by `scheduler/document-status.js`, is Up to date; a part ending within 30 days, as Workers' comp ends Nov 2, with the warning mark; or a part past its day, as Workers' comp expired, with the error mark; parts in the same state are named together, a kind with no parts says Ends Nov 2 or Expired, and a document with no end date says nothing. A row opens its file in a new tab through `documents.html?open=<id>`, which signs a ten-minute link and goes to it, so the browser's own viewer shows the file with its download and print buttons; Edit opens `documents.html?id=`, and Upload `documents.html?new`. A document is a file, a PDF, JPEG or PNG up to 20 MB, and six facts: its kind, the holder, the name printed on it, which a certificate's holder has without being a customer, the customer it belongs to, both empty for one that is for everyone, the driver, asked for only by a kind kept once for each driver, the day it ends and a note. A kind with parts, as a certificate has its policies, asks a day for each part in place of Ends, and a part left empty is one the document does not cover. A new file named for its holder, the holder first and then an underscore, fills an empty Holder, and then an empty Customer with the customer the holder's current document is linked to or, failing that, the customer of the holder's own name. Upload takes several files at once: each becomes its own document with the form's kind, end dates and note, its holder and customer worked out the same way, and a file that lands leaves the list, so a Save that stops partway is finished by the next. Choosing a new file on a saved document and saving puts a new copy in its place and keeps the one before as an old copy, which Show old lists; Delete is for a wrong upload and brings the copy before back. Kinds opens every kind to rename, mark as one for each driver, or add to. A customer's page lists its current documents on its Documents tab, each with the holder named on it where that is not the customer's own name, its status and its note, and each opening its file, with Upload and Open in Documents under them, which open this page for that customer; its Details tab ticks the kinds of driver form the customer asks for, under Driver forms required, and a customer a document names is not deleted. A driver's page lists the driver's current documents on its Documents tab, each with its status and note and opening its file, then each kind kept once for each driver that is not on file, saying None on file and opening the upload with the driver and kind filled in; a new document saved from that upload goes back to the tab, and while a Licence copy or Medical card is on file the Details tab's date it sets is not typed and links to it. |
| Itineraries | later | Needs the itinerary editor, an app component, and the AI intake. |
| Settings (`Options`) | keep, trimmed | `settings.html` holds the rates, the rate by month and the trips' limits, the late-return hour among them, and on its Office tab the yard, the needs and the billing steps. Saved locations have their own page, `locations.html`. |

| Samsara link | new | `live.html`, the Live map, after Schedule in the side nav: the buses out on a trip on a street map, with a panel of tiles beside it, or under it on a phone, as the Tasks pane's are. A bus out is its number on the trip's colour with an arrow the way it heads while it moves; its tile under On the road says the destination, the driver at the wheel by first name with the hours they may still drive, the speed, and the next stop. Every other bus Samsara has is a small tile under Yard, off the map, with its engine, or when it last reported. A pin rings with the light its bar casts, in its hue and rhythm, amber while the bus will be late and dashed grey with no signal, as much as the board's Motion lets it. Pressing a bus shows its leg: the path driven since it left the yard, its stops, filled once reached, and a dashed line on to the next; `live.html?bus=` opens on one bus. Asked once a minute while in view; the tiles follow the theme. |

The side nav lists only pages that exist; each view is added when its page is. Dropped: Game and Gallery links.

## 7. Where a surface lives

Three homes, and one rule for choosing.

| Home | What goes there |
|---|---|
| **The panel** | The detail of the thing you opened. One region right of the board, one open at a time. |
| **A page** | A list, a feed or a workspace: something you navigate to. |
| **A menu or a modal** | Options that change how the page draws, and one-off actions. |

- **Trip detail goes in the panel.** One editing surface beats two. A driver, a bus, a contact, a
  customer and a location are each edited on their own page, `drivers.html?id=`, `fleet.html?id=`,
  `contacts.html?id=`, `customers.html?id=` and `locations.html?id=`, because each is a list you go
  to and its record opens from that list; Design's `list-page.html` and `record-page.html` templates
  are the shape they all take, and `scheduler/pair.js` what they do alike.
- **A driver's and a bus's page list their upcoming trips,** each marked, before Save, where the
  page's time off, days out or Inactive would leave it without them: Time off then or Set Inactive
  on a driver's, Unit is out of service then or Unit is set Inactive on a bus's.
- **History is a page.** It is the detail of nothing, and last in the nav: it is consulted, not

### The driver availability grid

A compact grid left of the board, turned on by the toolbar's Driver
availability toggle. Dispatch reads the schedule and availability together, so
it is a companion view, not panel content, and it stays on until turned off.

| | |
|---|---|
| **In the trip panel** | Nothing. The panel carries the trip's own drivers. |
| **On the Drivers page** | The same grid, nothing highlighted. |
| **A popover** | The per-driver card (photo, city, phone, licence and medical expiry), from a cell. |

The grid and the panel can both be open. Only the panel is one at a time.

The grid is a table edge to edge in the pane, its day band right under the
pane's head and level with the board's, with the drivers in priority order:
a rule parts one priority from the next, and a name's title says its
priority. With a trip selected, its days are shaded in the header, and each
driver free on one of them has a mark there: a blue outlined, tinted bar when free every day,
and a warning-coloured one when free only some.

A driver's name is a toggle: pressed, it puts down the selected trip and its
card, every bar but that driver's dims on the board, and their name is set in
semibold on their own bars. Selecting one of their trips keeps them picked;
selecting a dimmed trip, the same name again, Escape, or hiding the grid puts
the board back. Right-click or hold a name for its menu: Send trips opens the

Call and Text, shown with a number, reach them as a trip's Contacts do; and
Pick out trips, or Show every trip while they are picked, is the name's own
press. A busy day selects its
trip, the next one each click on a day of two. A busy day after a trip the day
before shows the hours off between them, from the earlier trip's return to the
yard to the later one's departure: "15h", in the warning tone under 10 hours and the danger tone under 8,
"!" where the two overlap, and "?" until both trips have times.

### The trip bar does not expand

A bar growing on click re-stacks the lanes beside it. Its information is the
panel's job; its actions go in the panel header and on the bar's right-click
menu. On the compact board a block has no writing to grow into either: the
docked shortcut bar carries it at the bottom edge, and the block itself is the
same size selected or not.
