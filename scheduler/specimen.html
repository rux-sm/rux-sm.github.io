The toolbar, at each of its widths

The row never wraps. What gives way instead is the week's label, then Today. Each box below is the width its state begins at; on the live page the width is the schedule's own, which placeRoom measures, because the panels beside it take the room and a window cannot see them. Today answers the window as well: it leaves below Carbon’s md whatever the schedule’s own width, so the boxes below show it only on a window wider than that.

Wide: the whole week, every control

Under 25rem of toolbar: the week's months

The container query's own width. The full week stays as the button's accessible name, and the months take its place on screen.

Tight: Today moves to the menu

What data-room="tight" draws, at the 17rem schedule it is set for. Today leaves the row for the overflow menu, and the week's button gives up two steps of inset so its months read whole.

The compact board

Where the schedule cannot show three readable days it shows all seven, at the 375px of a phone. A trip is a 44px block in its colour carrying a code of up to three letters for its destination and nothing else, the same on every block however long it is. The day band numbers the days, and the selected trip's shortcut bar docks below with the rows the block gave up.
